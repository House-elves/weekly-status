# What's new

What changed for you in each version of Weekly status, newest first. The desk shows the parts that are new when it offers you an
update.

## 1.2.0

- New: voice input. 🎤 next to the status: say a line, and it goes in at the cursor. The desk listens, with your browser's speech recognition or Whisper on its
  machine. Needs Elf Desk 0.6.42.

## 1.1.0

- The status email is written by the coding agent you choose: Claude Code, Codex or pi, under **Coding agent** in its settings (the runner's choice unless you pick one). It needs runner 0.43.0; with an older runner it asks Claude Code as before.

## 1.0.2

- Safer: Claude writes the draft with no tools, none of your settings, and an empty folder.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed: it asks for Java 21 or newer, so it runs on the Java you have.

## 1.0.0

- Drafts your weekly status from your GitHub work. Edit it in its tab, send it to yourself, or put the team's copy in
  your Gmail drafts.
